# DEPI (Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative) — Overview

**Role:** Data Science Trainee
**Duration:** Oct 2024 – May 2025
**Status:** Completed

A longer-form data science training combining structured skill-building
(Python, data analysis, visualization, MLOps tooling) with hands-on,
team-based project delivery — owning feature engineering and model
evaluation within a 7-person team building end-to-end data pipelines for
AI model training.
