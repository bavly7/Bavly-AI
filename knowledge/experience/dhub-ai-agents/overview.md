# D-Hub — AI Agents & LLMs — Overview

**Program:** Master AI Agents & LLMs – Hands-On Training Program
**Collaboration:** Digital Hub (D-Hub) & Orange Digital Center
**Status:** IN PROGRESS (not yet completed)

Practical, project-based training on designing, deploying, and optimizing
AI-powered assistants using LangChain, Flowise, and LLMs.

## Curriculum
- AI Agents & LLM Ecosystem Fundamentals
- Prompt Engineering & Response Control
- Tool-Using Agents (Web, Math, APIs)
- Memory & Context Management
- Knowledge Integration (RAG with PDFs & Google Drive)
- Multi-Agent Collaboration Systems
- Automation with Emails, Sheets & APIs
- Deploying AI apps with web interfaces
- Debugging, Optimization & Performance Tuning

## Planned Projects
- Mid-term: AI Assistant that answers questions from PDFs or Google
  Sheets.
- Final: Full AI Agent System that retrieves data, summarizes it, and
  sends automated emails/reports, presented with a live demo.
