# Dhub AI Agents Training — Overview

**Role:** AI Agents Training Participant
**Program:** Master AI Agents & LLMs – Hands-On Training Program
**Collaboration:** Digital Hub (D-Hub) & Orange Digital Center
**Duration:** August 2026 – October 2026
**Status:** Completed

An intensive AI engineering training program focused on modern AI agent architectures, agentic systems, and production-oriented AI applications. The program covered structured data extraction, autonomous tool-using agents, LLM fine-tuning, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), conversational memory, multi-agent collaboration, and AI-powered backend applications using LangChain, LangGraph, and related frameworks.

The training progressed from foundational LLM concepts toward stateful, tool-using, database-connected, and user-facing AI applications, culminating in a fully autonomous multi-agent sales automation system.
