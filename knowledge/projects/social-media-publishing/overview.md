# Social Campaign Publisher — Overview

An AI-powered social media campaign tool that generates platform-specific
captions (Egyptian Arabic or English) from a single piece of source
content, schedules posts across four platforms (X, Instagram, Facebook,
LinkedIn), and publishes them — with a real, live LinkedIn integration and
safe sandboxed adapters for the rest. Built during a 20-week internship at
FlyRank, substantially exceeding the original scope of the assignment.

**GitHub:** `flyrank-capstone-social-studio`
**Tech stack:** Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Supabase, SQLAlchemy,
Alembic, Groq (openai/gpt-oss-120b), APScheduler, OAuth 2.0
