# Social Campaign Publisher — Problem

Publishing consistent, platform-appropriate social content across
multiple platforms is manual and repetitive: the same underlying message
needs different tone, length, hashtag density, and formatting per
platform, and scheduling/publishing across platforms typically means
juggling separate tools or manual posting.

This system solves that by taking one piece of source content and
generating tailored captions per platform automatically (in Egyptian
Arabic or English), scheduling them, and publishing them — while keeping
a human in the loop to review before anything goes live, so automation
doesn't come at the cost of brand safety.
