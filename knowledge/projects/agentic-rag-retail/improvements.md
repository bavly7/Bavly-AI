# Agentic RAG Retail Analytics — What Would Be Improved

- Multi-camera synchronization — coordinating analytics across more than
  one camera feed at once.
- Customer trajectory prediction — going beyond current zone/dwell-time
  tracking to predict where a customer is likely headed next.
- Shelf inventory monitoring — extending the CV pipeline beyond customer
  behavior to also track stock levels.
- Voice-enabled AI assistant — adding voice interaction on top of the
  current chat interface.
- Cloud deployment — moving from a local/demo setup to a real deployed
  environment.
- Real-time dashboard alerts — proactive notifications rather than only
  responding to manager-initiated queries.
