# Agentic RAG Retail Analytics — Motivation

Bavly wanted to empower supermarket owners to seamlessly access and
analyze their store's performance through a simple chat interface, rather
than staring at dashboards. Since he didn't have access to a live
supermarket backend, he simulated the environment — building a Computer
Vision model to process camera feeds (tracking customer movement, dwell
times in specific aisles, foot traffic) and a forecasting model to predict
sales outcomes based on hypothetical actions (e.g. applying a discount).

The goal was to build an AI partner that helps an owner make data-driven
decisions naturally through conversation, not a passive reporting tool.
