# LeadFlow AI — Motivation

Build a production-oriented autonomous sales pipeline demonstrating advanced agentic AI concepts: multi-agent orchestration, specialized agent roles, structured data passing, web scraping integration, and end-to-end automation without human intervention.

The goal was to create a fully autonomous system that handles the complete sales prospecting workflow from lead discovery through personalized outreach, showcasing the practical application of multi-agent collaboration in a real business context.
