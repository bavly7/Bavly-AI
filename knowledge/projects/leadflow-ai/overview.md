# LeadFlow AI — Overview

A fully autonomous multi-agent sales automation system that finds business leads, qualifies them using AI, and sends personalized cold sales emails without human intervention.

The system combines LangGraph multi-agent orchestration, Groq LLM, web scraping, Pydantic structured outputs, SQLite persistence, and SMTP automated email delivery to create an end-to-end sales pipeline.

**Three Specialized Agents:**
1. **Prospector**: Discovers business leads via web search
2. **Scraper & Qualifier**: Extracts contact info and qualifies leads using AI
3. **Closer**: Generates and sends personalized cold emails

**Tech Stack:** LangGraph, LangChain, Groq, Pydantic, SQLite, SMTP, Web Scraping
**Automation Level:** Fully autonomous end-to-end without human intervention
**Use Case:** AI chatbot service sales to restaurants and retail businesses
