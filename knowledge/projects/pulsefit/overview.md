# PulseFit — Overview

PulseFit is a web-based AI gym coach — a graduation project — that watches
a user train through their webcam, counts reps, corrects form in real
time with bilingual (Arabic/English) voice feedback, builds a
personalized workout program, and tracks nutrition, including Egyptian
and Franco-Arabic food logging with an LLM fallback for unknown foods.

**GitHub:** `Pulsefit`
**Tech stack:** Python, Flask, Ultralytics YOLO11-pose, MediaPipe Pose,
OpenCV, Groq, gTTS, SQLite, Docker
