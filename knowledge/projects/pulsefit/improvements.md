# PulseFit — What Would Be Improved (V2 Vision)

- Extend client-side MediaPipe pose estimation with live skeleton
  visualization to more exercises (already proven on hammer curl),
  rendering the skeleton overlay live for the user.
- Evolve the current rule-based recommender into an ML-powered engine
  trained from real user acceptance and progress data — which plans
  users actually stick with and progress on.
- AI-generated injury rehabilitation programs — adaptive plans for users
  training around an injury.
- Sport-specific conditioning plans tailored to a chosen sport's demands,
  not just general fitness goals.
- More natural, expressive voice coaching, replacing TTS-cached cues with
  something less robotic.
