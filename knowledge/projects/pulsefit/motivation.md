# PulseFit — Motivation

Bavly wanted to build something that genuinely helps people take care of
their health without it feeling like a rigid, impossible chore. The goal
wasn't to force a single "perfect" universal fitness standard on
everyone, but to find the healthiest possible routine that adapts to each
specific person's body, capabilities, and context. Fitness should adapt
to the user, not the other way around — that principle shaped the whole
system, from the deterministic-but-personalizing workout recommender to
supporting Arabic and Franco-Arabic food logging rather than assuming an
English-only, one-size-fits-all user.
