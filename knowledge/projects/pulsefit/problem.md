# PulseFit — Problem

Most people training without a coach either don't know if their exercise
form is correct (risking injury or reduced results) or don't have a
personalized workout plan matched to their actual experience level,
equipment, injuries, and goals — they end up following generic templates
that don't account for who they specifically are.

PulseFit solves this by watching the user train through a webcam, giving
real-time form correction the way a human coach would, and generating a
workout and nutrition plan personalized to the individual rather than a
one-size-fits-all program — while remaining accessible to Arabic and
Franco-Arabic speaking users specifically, not just English speakers.
