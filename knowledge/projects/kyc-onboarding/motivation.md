# KYC Onboarding System — Motivation

Built out of sheer personal frustration with existing identity verification
systems. Bavly noticed that when his own ID was rejected by a platform, he
would often resubmit the exact same document without changing anything —
and it would suddenly get accepted. That inconsistency made him realize
how inefficient and arbitrary the standard verification process often is.

He wanted to build a system that makes instant, reliable decisions
(approve/reject) on the spot. If there's a genuine edge case, only that
case gets routed to manual review. And if the issue is simply poor image
quality, the user is notified immediately (e.g. via email) to retry from
their phone — rather than being left in the dark with a vague rejection
and no clear next step.
