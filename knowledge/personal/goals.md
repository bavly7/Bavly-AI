# Goals

## Target Roles
Bavly is primarily looking for an opportunity to prove his skills and
deliver real value in a production environment. His strongest expertise
and primary targets are **AI Engineer**, **Applied AI (Agents) Engineer**,
and **Computer Vision Engineer** roles, followed closely by **Machine
Learning Engineer** positions.

## Industry / Domain
Industry-agnostic. No strict preference for fintech, healthcare, or
retail — the main focus is on the complexity of the problems being solved
and the opportunity to build impactful AI solutions.

## Location & Work Arrangement
Currently based in Giza, Egypt. Highly adaptable and open to remote
(international), hybrid, or on-site opportunities depending on the role's
requirements.

## Short-Term Goal (6–12 months)
Integrate into a real, fast-paced work environment where he can apply his
architectural and training skills to production-level challenges.

## Long-Term Direction
Continuously grow and take on more complex AI system design problems,
staying at the cutting edge of the field.

## What to Avoid
Roles strictly limited to data entry, basic analytics, or purely
theoretical research with no path to deployment. He wants to build and
ship real products.
