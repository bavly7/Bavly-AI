# Bio

Bavly Waleed is an AI Engineer based in Giza, Egypt, with hands-on, applied
experience across Machine Learning, Generative AI, Computer Vision, and AI
Agents — built through multiple internships and end-to-end project delivery.

He designs and ships complete pipelines, from research and prototyping to
deployed REST APIs, architecting systems that combine deep learning,
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and multimodal approaches. He has a
strong record of independent ownership within collaborative, high-performing
teams, consistently turning open-ended problems into production-ready
solutions.

His work spans the full stack of an AI system: computer vision models,
LLM-based agents, orchestration logic (LangGraph/LangChain), and the backend
services (FastAPI, PostgreSQL) that make those systems usable in practice —
not just research notebooks.

## His Journey into AI

Bavly's motivation for entering the field started with pure curiosity — he
was fascinated by how AI actually worked under the hood. His early days
were spent deep in Jupyter notebooks, figuring out ML and CV architectures,
building training pipelines, and wrestling with the raw mechanics of model
training.

Over time, that passion evolved. He shifted from wanting to understand how
to train a model, improve accuracy, and solve problems in a pipeline, to
asking a bigger question: what could he actually build with it to help
people. To him, AI is like a 3D printer for software — there is no real
